### 2. 🧮 Mathematical & Formula Queries

#### Q5: "What is the mathematical formula for Scaled Dot-Product Attention, including the scaling factor $\sqrt{d_k}$?"
* **Expected Ground Truth**:
  $$\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V$$
* **Reference**: Section 3.2.1, Equation (1) (Page 4).

#### Q6: "Write the formulas used to calculate Positional Encodings using sine and cosine functions."
* **Expected Ground Truth**:
  $$PE_{(pos, 2i)} = \sin\left(\frac{pos}{10000^{2i/d_{model}}}\right)$$
  $$PE_{(pos, 2i+1)} = \cos\left(\frac{pos}{10000^{2i/d_{model}}}\right)$$
  where $pos$ is the position and $i$ is the dimension index.
* **Reference**: Section 3.5 (Page 6).

#### Q7: "What is the equation for Multi-Head Attention using matrix projection weights $W_i^Q, W_i^K, W_i^V$?"
* **Expected Ground Truth**:
  $$\text{MultiHead}(Q, K, V) = \text{Concat}(\text{head}_1, \dots, \text{head}_h)W^O$$
  $$\text{where } \text{head}_i = \text{Attention}(QW_i^Q, KW_i^K, VW_i^V)$$
  Projections: $W_i^Q \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{model} \times d_k}$, $W_i^K \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{model} \times d_k}$, $W_i^V \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{model} \times d_v}$, $W^O \in \mathbb{R}^{hd_v \times d_{model}}$.
* **Reference**: Section 3.2.2, Equations (2) & (3) (Page 4-5).

#### Q8: "What formula defines the Position-wise Feed-Forward Network (FFN) with ReLU activation?"
* **Expected Ground Truth**:
  $$\text{FFN}(x) = \max(0, xW_1 + b_1)W_2 + b_2$$
  Linear transformation from $d_{model}=512$ to inner layer $d_{ff}=2048$ and back to $d_{model}=512$.
* **Reference**: Section 3.3 (Page 5).

#### Q11: "Where are Add & Norm (residual connection followed by layer normalization) placed in the architecture diagram?"

#### Q12: "How does the Masked Multi-Head Attention block prevent positions from attending to subsequent tokens?"
* **Expected Ground Truth**:
  * By masking out (setting to $-\infty$) all values in the input of the softmax corresponding to illegal connections (positions $j > i$).
  * This ensures predictions for position $i$ depend only on known outputs at positions less than $i$ (auto-regressive property).
* **Reference**: Section 3.2.3 (Page 5).

### 4. 🔀 Hybrid / Cross-Modal Reasoning Queries

#### Q13: "Why is Scaled Dot-Product Attention divided by $\sqrt{d_k}$ when $d_k$ is large, according to both text and mathematical justification?"
* **Expected Ground Truth**:
  * For large values of $d_k$, the dot products grow large in magnitude, pushing the softmax function into regions that have extremely small gradients (vanishing gradient problem).
  * Assuming $q$ and $k$ are independent random variables with mean 0 and variance 1, their dot product $q \cdot k = \sum_{i=1}^{d_k} q_i k_i$ has mean 0 and variance $d_k$. Dividing by $\sqrt{d_k}$ scales the variance back to 1.
* **Reference**: Section 3.2.1 Footnote 4 (Page 4).

#### Q14: "How does self-attention compare to recurrent and convolutional layers in terms of maximum path length and per-layer complexity?"
* **Expected Ground Truth**:
  * **Self-Attention**: Complexity per layer $O(n^2 \cdot d)$, Sequential operations $O(1)$, Maximum Path Length $O(1)$.
  * **Recurrent (RNN)**: Complexity per layer $O(n \cdot d^2)$, Sequential operations $O(n)$, Maximum Path Length $O(n)$.
  * **Convolutional**: Complexity per layer $O(k \cdot n \cdot d^2)$, Sequential operations $O(1)$, Maximum Path Length $O(\log_k(n))$.
* **Reference**: Table 1 & Section 4 (Page 6).

#### Q15: "Explain how positional encodings allow the model to generalize to sequence lengths longer than those seen during training."
* **Expected Ground Truth**:
  * Because $PE_{pos+k}$ can be represented as a linear function of $PE_{pos}$ for any fixed offset $k$. Sinusoidal encodings allow extrapolation beyond training lengths.
* **Reference**: Section 3.5 (Page 6).
