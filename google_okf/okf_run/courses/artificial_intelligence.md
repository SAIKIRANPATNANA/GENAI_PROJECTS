# Artificial Intelligence Foundations

**Course code:** AI201

## Overview

A broad survey course covering search algorithms, knowledge representation, planning, and an overview of the subfields that make up modern AI, including machine learning and deep learning.

## Prerequisites

None formally, though basic programming familiarity is recommended.

## Learning Outcomes

- Describe the major subfields of artificial intelligence
- Understand fundamental search and planning algorithms
- Articulate how machine learning and deep learning fit within the broader field of AI

## Notes

This survey course positions [Machine Learning](./machine_learning.md) and [Deep Learning](./deep_learning.md) as subfields of the broader discipline of Artificial Intelligence, rather than as courses that are prerequisites for it.
