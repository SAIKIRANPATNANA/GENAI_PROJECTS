# Curriculum Examination & Grading Rules

← [Back to Index](../index.md)

## Overview

Administrative policy document covering examination structure, grading, and attendance requirements for the AI/Cybersecurity program. Contains **no course-content or subject-matter information**.

## Grading Structure

| Component | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Coursework | 40% |
| Final Exam | 60% |
| **Pass Mark** | **50%** |

## Attendance

- **Minimum 75% attendance** is required to sit final examinations.
- Students who fall below this threshold are barred from finals.

## Resit Policy

- Students are permitted **one resit opportunity** per failed module.
- Resit marks are **capped at the pass mark** (i.e., a maximum of 50%).

## Notes

This is a pure policy document. It does not relate to any specific course's content, prerequisites, or subject relationships.
