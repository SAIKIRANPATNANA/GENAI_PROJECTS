# Artificial Intelligence Foundations

**Course Code:** AI201
← [Back to Index](../index.md)

## Overview

A broad survey course that maps the full landscape of modern AI — search algorithms, knowledge representation, planning, and an introduction to the major subfields including machine learning and deep learning. Intended as an orienting course for the program.

## Topics Covered

- Search algorithms (BFS, DFS, A*, heuristic search)
- Knowledge representation and reasoning
- Planning and constraint satisfaction
- Overview of machine learning and deep learning as subfields of AI

## Prerequisites

None formally, though basic programming familiarity is recommended.

## Learning Outcomes

- Describe the major subfields of artificial intelligence
- Understand fundamental search and planning algorithms
- Articulate how machine learning and deep learning fit within the broader field of AI

## Relationship to Other Courses

> **Key structural note:** This course treats [Machine Learning](machine_learning.md) and [Deep Learning](deep_learning.md) as *subfields* it surveys — not as prerequisites for itself. This is an important distinction from the rest of the curriculum, where ML/DL are sequential prerequisites.

| Course | Code | Relationship |
|--------|------|-------------|
| [Machine Learning](machine_learning.md) | AI301 | Surveyed here as a subfield; runs independently in the curriculum |
| [Deep Learning](deep_learning.md) | AI401 | Surveyed here as a subfield; runs independently in the curriculum |

## Notes

AI201 is a conceptual survey — it positions the student in the field before they go deep on any one track. It does not require the quantitative depth of STAT201 or AI301.
