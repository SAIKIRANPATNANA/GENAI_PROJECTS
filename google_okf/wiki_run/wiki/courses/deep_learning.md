# Deep Learning

**Course Code:** AI401
← [Back to Index](../index.md)

## Overview

Introduces neural network architectures and the mathematical machinery that trains them — backpropagation, gradient-based optimization, and regularization. Also provides a first look at CNNs and RNNs that the specialization electives will extend.

## Topics Covered

- Feedforward neural networks and the perceptron model
- Backpropagation and gradient descent (SGD, Adam, etc.)
- Regularization techniques (dropout, weight decay, batch norm)
- Intro to Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)
- Intro to Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)

## Prerequisites

| Course | Code | Reason |
|--------|------|--------|
| [Machine Learning](machine_learning.md) | AI301 | Must already understand supervised learning, model evaluation, and optimization basics |

## Learning Outcomes

- Implement and train feedforward neural networks
- Apply backpropagation and gradient-based optimization
- Use regularization techniques to control overfitting
- Understand the basic building blocks of CNNs and RNNs

## Notes

Deep Learning is the **bridge course** between classical ML and the application-specific electives. The neural network concepts introduced here underpin both specialization tracks (CV and NLP).
