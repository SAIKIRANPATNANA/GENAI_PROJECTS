# Natural Language Processing

**Course Code:** AI412
← [Back to Index](../index.md)

## Overview

Specialization elective extending the RNN and attention foundations from Deep Learning into full NLP pipelines — text preprocessing, embeddings, sequence modeling, and transformer architectures. Highly relevant for LLM and generative AI applications.

## Topics Covered

- Text preprocessing (tokenization, normalization, padding)
- Word embeddings (Word2Vec, GloVe) and sentence embeddings
- Sequence models (RNNs, LSTMs, GRUs)
- Attention mechanisms and self-attention
- Transformer architectures (foundational to BERT, GPT, etc.)

## Prerequisites

| Course | Code | Reason |
|--------|------|--------|
| [Deep Learning](deep_learning.md) | AI401 | Extends neural network and RNN foundations into sequence/attention architectures |

## Learning Outcomes

- Build text classifiers using embeddings
- Work with sequence models and attention mechanisms
- Implement core components of transformer architectures

## Parallel Specialization Track

> NLP and [Computer Vision](computer_vision.md) (AI411) are **parallel electives** — both build directly on Deep Learning, but neither depends on the other.

## Notes

One of two terminal specialization electives. Transformer architectures covered here are the direct precursors to modern LLMs (GPT, BERT, Gemini, etc.), making this course especially relevant for students interested in AI security and generative AI.
