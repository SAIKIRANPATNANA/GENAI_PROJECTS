# Curriculum Examination & Grading Rules

**Source document:** curriculum_rules.md

## Overview

Administrative policy document covering examination structure, grading bands, and attendance requirements for the AI/Cybersecurity program.

## Grading

Coursework counts for 40% of the final mark and the final exam counts for 60%. The pass mark is 50%.

## Attendance

A minimum of 75% attendance is required to sit final examinations.

## Resits

Students are permitted one resit opportunity per failed module. Resit marks are capped at the pass mark.

## Notes

This document contains no information about course content, prerequisites, or subject relationships.
